        03 · Audience

        People at a turning point

        She named five groups. The thread through all of them: someone senior, mid-change, who needs both rigour and depth.

        Senior leaders

        New mandate, bigger room, fewer people to be honest with.

        Founders

        The business has grown past the way they lead it.

        Women at a turning point

        A shift in life, not only career, asking who they are now.

        Family business heirs

        Carrying a name while finding their own way to lead.

        Changing countries or roles

        Everything familiar left behind at once.

        Who decides first

        All five stay open. Month three of the content plan tests which one answers.

        How clients arrive

        Through referral, from third and fourth level connections. Her close circle won't be her clients: coaching asks people to unmask, and friends won't do that with her. So they become champions who speak about her instead.

        Small on purpose

        Around ten clients is the ceiling she wants. The first goal is ten discovery calls. Global and online, likely Indian and of a similar life stage.

        05 · Offers

        Ways to work together

        Discovery call

        45 minutes, free, on Calendly with a video link. “What's possible for you, and whether this feels like the right partnership.”

        Six-Session Coaching Journey

        Six 60-minute sessions, ideally fortnightly. In person, online or hybrid.

        C-level one to one

        A six-month engagement for CEOs and senior executives. 60 minutes, fortnightly.

        Team coaching

        8 to 12 people, in person, one session a month for six months. Feedback, leadership visibility, delegation.

        Prices are never public. Every offer says “Enquire”, a standing policy since 20 Oct.
