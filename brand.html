Feed style, v1

  • Mix the formats. Photo, carousel, single text post. Never three carousels in a row.
  • Photos: her real photos, full frame, one short line in Jost. No text bands.
  • Text posts: Jost on sage wash, left aligned, plain. No logo on every slide.
  • The Within Question: forest green, Fraunces italic. The only place the serif headline appears.
  • Carousels: three slides, one thought each. The caption carries the story.
  • Apart from the launch look: no split layouts, pills or badges.
