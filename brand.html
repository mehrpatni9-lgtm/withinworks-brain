House style

Always

  • Forest or paper grounds, the serif doing the work
  • Real, everyday photographs and offset cards
  • The W bottom left; with the full name on LinkedIn
  • One idea per slide, credited when it is someone else’s
  • A quiet close: “Let’s have a conversation.”

Never

  • Listicles, pills, swipe arrows, infographics
  • Exclamation marks, superlatives, hustle
  • Hard sells or booking language
  • Stock clichés and decorative glows
