Reels

Megha, in her own words

Ten reels, none repeating a post. Most are conversations: questions about her life she already knows the answers to. Tap a reel to open it.

The idea

Posts carry the ideas. Reels carry Megha. Nothing on a reel repeats a post: no “should”, no coach versus mentor. Reels are where people hear her voice, her stories and her laugh, and decide she’s someone they’d like to talk to.

How we film

  • Mostly conversations. Mehr asks a question off camera, Megha answers in her own words, and we cut the best 60 to 90 seconds. Nothing to learn by heart; they’re questions about her own life.
  • A few directed reels. Short, planned shots with a line or two.
  • One morning a month. Phone, window light, her home or office.

Five formats

Conversation · 1 to 2 minutes

Ask Megha

One question about her life, answered honestly. Her career, her mistakes, being a woman leader in the Middle East.

Directed · 1 to 2 minutes

Coaching, live

A real, short coaching moment on a small topic, filmed over the shoulder. Shows what no post can.

Conversation · 20 to 30 seconds

One answer

A single question, a single story. Travel, a turning point, a question that changed things.

Directed · 15 to 20 seconds

Light moments

Quickfire this-or-that, and her dog. Warmth, so the feed never feels heavy.

Voiceover · 20 to 30 seconds

A coach’s morning

Her real routine in quiet shots, with her voice over it.

What the 140 posts taught us

  • Origin stories land. “I became a coach to help others. I stayed because it transformed me” was one of ICF’s most liked posts.
  • Ex-executives who became coaches are a theme people follow. Megha can tell it first-hand.
  • Lived story beats bought frameworks. One of the references you sent made exactly that point.
  • Most coaching pages describe coaching. Few show it. One of ours shows a real coaching moment.

L5 · Coaching, liveDirected · 1:00 to 1:30
Why it’s not a postShows a real coaching moment instead of describing one.

How it works

  • A volunteer (Mehr, or a friend who agrees) brings a small, real, low-stakes topic. A weekend decision, a habit they want to change.
  • Megha coaches for five minutes. We cut the best 90 seconds.
  • Nothing private, and the volunteer signs off the final cut.

What we keep

A question that makes the volunteer stop and think, and the pause after it.

Shot

Two chairs, filmed over the volunteer’s shoulder so we see Megha listening.

On screen · caption

Small labels as it happens: “She asks, she doesn’t tell”, “She reflects it back”, “The pause”.

What five minutes of coaching actually looks like.

One filming morning

  1. L1 to L4, S3 and S5 in one sitting, about 60 to 90 minutes. Mehr asks, Megha talks.
  2. S1 Quickfire straight after, while she’s warmed up.
  3. S2 and S4 at home, the same morning.
  4. L5 on a separate day, with a volunteer.

Rhythm

  • One reel a week, a longer one and a short one in turn.
  • First month: S1, L1, S2, L2.
  • What we watch: saves, shares, replies and DMs, and discovery calls. Not views.

When she’s ready
