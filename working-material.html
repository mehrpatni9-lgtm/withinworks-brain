The sheets used in sessions, in the order of the Within series.

How the sessions use them

The work follows the Within series. The Within Compass shows where you are now. The Within Core names what matters most. The inner voices sheet shows what each old habit of mind costs those values, and Within Direction is the next step you choose. The client leads; curiosity comes before advice, and every session ends with one thing to notice or do that week.
