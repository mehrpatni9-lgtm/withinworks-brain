The sheets Megha uses with clients, in the order she uses them.

How the sessions use them

The work starts with the Wheel of Life to see where you are, then the Values Sheet to name what matters, then the Saboteurs Worksheet, where your top three values show what each inner voice costs you. The client leads; curiosity comes before advice, and every session ends with one thing you will notice or do that week.

Print, A4

Values Sheet

Circle the words that pull you, narrow to five, then write your top three in your own words with a moment you lived each one. Ends on: which value am I honouring least right now?

Saboteurs Worksheet

The old strategies that once kept you safe and now steer without permission: Controller, Pleaser, Hyper-Achiever, Restless, or your own. For each, when it shows up, what it says, what it costs and which value it sacrifices. Then: meeting your Inner Leader.
