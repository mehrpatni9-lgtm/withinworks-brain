Podcast

Megha and Geet, in conversation

Two careers, one home, and the turns that led here. Filmed side by side, asking each other.

How it flows

  • About 30 minutes. Megha asks first, then Geet. Megha has the last word.
  • Read the questions once together, then talk freely.
  • Answer with a moment, not a summary.

Megha asks Geet

What did you really think when I told you I wanted to become a coach?

When we were building the company, what did you see in me that I couldn’t see myself?

Which year tested us the most, and what got us through it?

Hot take

Should couples build a business together?

What kind of father did you imagine you’d be? And what kind are you?

Who are the friends who carried us through the hard years?

What have you changed about the way you lead?

What do you think I’m still learning?

Geet asks Megha

A short hint under each, so you can prep each other.

We had built something successful. Why coaching?

The urge to build something of your own, the turbulence, the personal moment that made you stop. Then the three days of CPCC fundamentals in 2022.

Banking, a company of 300, and now coaching. What did each chapter teach you?

One lesson per chapter, said simply.

What did you have to unlearn?

The planner who got upset when plans changed. Moving from having the answers to asking the questions.

Who taught you the most about leadership?

Your father, his journey as an entrepreneur, and how he looked at life in his last phase.

What has raising our daughters taught you about leading people?

Patience, and letting them find their own answers. The coach in you started at home.

You came to Dubai 24 years ago. What did you miss, and what did you find?

Starting over in a new country, building friendships from scratch, the moment it became home.

Would you ever call anywhere other than Dubai home?

An honest answer, even an unsure one.

Hot take

Do leaders need more answers, or better questions?

Your line: “Most leaders don’t need answers. They need better questions.”

What do people get wrong about coaching?

A process, not a pill. Different from advice and from therapy.
