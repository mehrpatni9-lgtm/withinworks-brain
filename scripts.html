Prep notes for recorded conversations. Natural, never word for word.

Podcast

Megha and Geet, in conversation

Two careers, one home, and the turns that led here. Filmed side by side, asking each other.

How it flows

  • About 30 minutes. Megha asks first, then Geet. Megha has the last word.
  • Read the questions once together, then talk freely.
  • Answer with a moment, not a summary.

Megha asks Geet

What did you really think when I told you I wanted to become a coach?

When we were building the company, what did you see in me that I couldn’t see myself?

Which year tested us the most, and what got us through it?

Hot take

Should couples build a business together?

What kind of father did you imagine you’d be? And what kind are you?

What mindsets carried us through the hard years?

What’s something you’ve changed your mind about in the last few years?

What do you think I’m still learning?

Geet asks Megha

A short hint under each, so you can prep each other.

We’d built something we were proud of, and then you chose coaching. What was really going on for you then?

The urge to build something of your own, the turbulence, the personal moment that made you stop. Then the three days of CPCC fundamentals in 2022.

Banking, the company, now coaching. Which chapter did you love most?

Say why, with one moment from it.

You were always the planner in our house. What happened to her?

Getting upset when plans changed, then learning how little is really in our control.

You talk about your dad a lot. What would he say if he saw you now?

His journey as an entrepreneur, and how he looked at life in his last phase.

Be honest. Are you coaching the girls at home too?

Patience, and letting them find their own answers. Laugh at yourself a little.

We came to Dubai 24 years ago. Was there ever a time you didn’t want to live here?

The early years, missing home, the friends who became family, the moment it felt like ours. Would anywhere else ever feel like home?

Hot take

Should friends give advice nobody asked for?

Telling versus asking. Why a good question often helps more than good advice.

What do people get wrong about what you do now?

A process, not a pill. Different from advice and from therapy.

The close

What’s one thing going right in your life right now, and one thing that could be better?
