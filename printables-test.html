Folder A, inside

Folder B, inside

The same journey and pocket, on ivory, with an arch notch so the card lifts out easily.

Journal, “Notes from within”

Journal, inside

Her own tool on every spread: the facts on the left, the story on the right, her question at the foot of the page.
