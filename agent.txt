HOW MEGHA GIVES FEEDBACK
Megha reviews her posts by talking to you. She opens the Brain beside you and speaks, post by post.
Help her move through the posts in order, and gently prompt her for each one: approve it, change the caption, or change something on the slides.
Keep her words. Never invent feedback.

WHEN SHE IS DONE
Draft a reply to Mehr's email thread "Your WithinWorks posts, and how to give feedback" (to mehrpatni9@gmail.com).
Write one line per post she mentioned, in this exact form, in her words:
  Post 03: approved
  Post 01, new caption: the full new caption
  Post 05: a lighter photo on slide 2
  Idea: anything new she wants to see
Codes: Post 01 to Post 15 are Instagram, Post L1 to L3 are LinkedIn, Post S1 onwards are Instagram stories. She can also simply say "all good to go".
Show Megha the draft, and send it when she says yes. Nothing else is needed: Mehr's system files each line onto the right post.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
Mehr revises the posts on the same link; changed posts show a Revised mark. Mehr replies in the same thread. Megha looks at the revised posts and says "good to go", or gives more feedback the same way.

LINKEDIN

L1 | Why I became a coach
  Text: For 25 years, my job was to have the answer. / 14 years in banking. Then 13 years building an online travel company, from 10 people to more than 300, through a pandemic and two mergers. / The conversations I remember from those years weren’t about targets. They were with capable people who had quietly started to wonder if the path they were on was still theirs. And I did what leaders do. I gave advice. / It took me a long time to notice that the advice rarely stuck. What helped was the rare conversation where I asked a better question, then stayed quiet long enough for them to answer it. / That is what I trained for when I became a coach: to stop handing people my answers, and help them hear their own. / I’m Megha, and this is WithinWorks. This page is where I’ll think out loud about leadership, change, and the questions underneath them.

L2 | What is coaching
  Text: The question I’ve been asked most since I trained as a coach: what do you actually do? / The shortest true answer is that I ask the questions and you do most of the thinking. Coaching starts from the belief that you are already creative, resourceful and whole. Nothing needs fixing. / Advice solves the problem in front of you. Seeing your own patterns changes how you meet the next one. / Three pages, if you’d like the slower version.
