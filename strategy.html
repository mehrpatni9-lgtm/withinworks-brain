Strategy

The quiet authority

How WithinWorks Leadership becomes the coach senior people think of first, without ever raising its voice.

The idea

Most coaches talk about coaching. Megha will let people feel what a better question does.

She is a seasoned leader, now a coach: 25 years on the other side of the table, trained in Co-Active coaching. Every post either asks a question worth sitting with, or shows what changes when someone does. Calm, direct, never loud.
