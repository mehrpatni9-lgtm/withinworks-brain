The space only Megha can own

Most coaches talk about leadership from the coaching chair. Megha spent twenty-five years in the other one: the people chair. She hired, promoted, let people go, and held a company together through a pandemic.

Leaders told her things they never said in the boardroom. So every post speaks from both chairs: what she saw while leading people, and what she now helps them see in themselves.

Four threads, and what sits in each

The Within Question

Her signature. One question a week, numbered like an edition, never answered. After twelve, a small booklet people can keep.

  • Whose voice do you hear when you say “I should”?
  • What are you calling patience that is really fear?
  • Which conversation have you rehearsed more than you have had it?
  • Who are you when the title comes off?

From the people chair

What leading people taught her. Real moments, never named or placeable.

  • Leading people who are frightened
  • The person I got wrong
  • The question I asked in every interview
  • The only woman in the room

Inside the room

What coaching is, how a session feels, and what she is learning as a coach.

  • What happens in a first conversation
  • Why coaching, after twenty-five years
  • ACC, as a quiet update
  • A client’s words, with their OK

Outside the room

The person behind the practice, shown lightly.

  • The first twenty minutes of my day
  • A page I am reading
  • Travel, yoga and meditation
  • Her father’s story, when she is ready

How we work, in threes

  1. We make three posts from the threads above.
  2. Megha approves or comments on the Content page.
  3. We post them and share them on WhatsApp where it fits.
  4. We read what landed: saves, replies, discovery calls.
  5. The next three, sharper for it.

Where it goes

  • Instagram leads. Posts, short reels, stories when something real happens.
  • LinkedIn gets the same idea, rewritten for leaders.
  • WhatsApp: a short note to her circle, about once a month.
  • We measure discovery calls, replies and saves. Followers come second.
  • Later: a small paid test from January, then podcasts and forums she picks.
